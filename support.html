Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

A: To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Select CleanPlay
  5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"
Q: Are my photos uploaded to a server?

A: No! All photo analysis happens on your device. Your photos never leave your phone.

Q: Can I recover deleted photos?

A: Check your iPhone's "Recently Deleted" album. Photos stay there for 30 days before permanent deletion.
